About Sign Language Translator

This app uses a real MediaPipe hand-tracking model to find 21 points on your hand. Geometric rules and your saved examples are used to recognize supported static hand shapes.

Supported shapes

I, L, U, V, W, Y, and the “I love you” hand shape. You can teach the app additional personal static poses.

Requirements

A camera, a browser with WebGL graphics support, and a secure HTTPS page or localhost. To run locally: python3 -m http.server 8000, then open http://localhost:8000.

Limitations

This is not a full ASL interpreter. It does not recognize continuous signing, moving letters such as J and Z, facial grammar, or two-handed signs. Personal examples cannot distinguish poses differing only by orientation.

“Shape match” measures geometric similarity. It is not a measured accuracy percentage. Recognition accuracy has not been validated with a real signer study.

Privacy

Video is processed in this browser and is not recorded or uploaded. Examples and saved text use local browser storage. Model files are included with the app. Read aloud uses the browser’s speech service, which may process text remotely.
